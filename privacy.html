给家长的一份说明

隐私说明

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 26 日

开发中 · 尚未发布

「汉字小岛：趣味识字」目前处于规划与视觉设计阶段。下述游戏相关说明描述当前设计，并非对已发行产品的验证结果。正式发布前，我们会核对实际构建、权限、依赖及数据处理，并更新本说明。网站和支持邮件部分适用于您现在访问本站或联系支持时。

汉字小岛由 Peng Lin 开发，计划面向 5～7 岁儿童及陪伴他们的家长。隐私问题请由家长或监护人联系 540933120@qq.com。

1. 账号与儿童信息

当前游戏设计无需注册或登录，不要求儿童提供姓名、生日、照片、联系方式或其他身份信息。游戏内不设计聊天、公开个人资料或局域网多人功能。家长区计划在支持联系、外部链接及重要设置前设置家长验证。

2. 设备本地记录

计划在设备本地保存识字学习记录、关卡进度和声音设置，用于继续学习、展示进度及恢复偏好。当前不设计开发者业务服务器、账号服务或开发者云同步；这些学习记录不计划通过应用内采集服务发送给开发者。

iOS App 的本地数据和浏览器试玩版本（如后续提供）的浏览器数据将分别保存在各自环境中，不应视为自动互通。系统备份可能包含或恢复本地数据，具体取决于设备设置和最终实现。

3. 广告、追踪与设备权限

当前设计不接入第三方广告、追踪或统计 SDK，不使用广告标识符，也不设计申请相机、麦克风、定位、联系人或局域网权限的功能。我们会在正式发布前核查实际依赖和权限，若设计变化，将相应更新说明。

4. 设备与平台服务

Apple 可能依据其政策及您的设备设置处理下载、设备诊断、崩溃或其他平台数据，并可能向开发者提供平台允许的报告。这与游戏主动上传学习记录不同。本说明不表示 Apple 或操作系统完全不处理数据。相关控制请查看设备隐私设置及 Apple 隐私政策。

5. 支持邮件

当家长主动发送支持邮件时，开发者会收到发件邮箱、邮件内容及自愿提供的附件，用于回复、排查问题及处理隐私请求。请不要发送儿童的姓名、照片、学校、住址、账号密码或其他不必要的敏感信息。

邮件会经过您使用的邮件服务及支持邮箱服务（QQ 邮箱），并受各自服务政策约束。我们仅在处理请求和必要后续事项所需期间保留支持信息，法律要求保留的情况除外。您可以联系同一邮箱，要求查阅、更正或删除直接发给支持的信息；邮件服务的备份与保留机制可能另有规则。

6. 管理与删除记录

本地学习记录在设备上管理，开发者无法从业务服务器查询或远程删除这些记录，因为当前未设计此类服务器。删除 iOS App 通常会移除其本地数据；仅“卸载 App”（保留文稿与数据的系统操作）可能保留数据。清除浏览器的游戏网站数据可能删除浏览器版本的进度。系统备份可能保留旧副本或在恢复设备时还原记录，请同时检查备份设置。正式版本的具体操作将在发布前核对。

如您认为儿童向支持邮箱发送了不必要的个人信息，请由家长联系上述邮箱，以便我们协助处理删除请求。

7. 本支持网站与外部链接

本网站使用 GitHub Pages 托管。访问页面会向托管服务发送请求，包含 IP 地址、浏览器信息等网络请求数据。GitHub 可能依据其 隐私声明处理请求和日志；我们不能承诺托管方不保留日志。

本站未加入广告、分析或追踪脚本、外部字体、嵌入式媒体或联系表单。网站不主动读取游戏的本地学习记录。访问网站、打开外部政策链接或发送邮件需要网络连接；外部服务按各自政策处理数据。

8. 说明更新与联系

如产品设计或数据处理发生变化，我们会在本页更新内容和日期。正式发布前的最终说明应与实际版本一致。开发者：Peng Lin；支持与隐私联系：540933120@qq.com。

A note for parents

Privacy notice

Last updated: September 26, 2026

In development · Not yet released

Hanzi Island is currently in planning and visual design. The game-related statements below describe the current design, not verified behavior of a released product. Before release, we will review the actual build, permissions, dependencies, and data handling and update this notice. The website and support email sections apply when you visit this site or contact support now.

Hanzi Island (汉字小岛：趣味识字) is developed by Peng Lin and is planned for children aged 5–7 with a parent or guardian. Adults can send privacy questions to 540933120@qq.com.

1. Accounts and children's information

The current game design requires no registration or login, and does not ask children for a name, birth date, photo, contact details, or other identifying information. In-game chat, public profiles, and local-network multiplayer are not planned. A parent area with an adult check is planned before support contact, external links, and important settings.

2. Records on the device

Character learning records, level progress, and sound settings are planned to be saved locally to resume learning, display progress, and restore preferences. No developer-operated backend, account service, or cloud sync is currently planned. These learning records are not intended to be sent to the developer through an in-app collection service.

Local data in the iOS app and browser data in a browser demo, if offered later, would be stored separately in their respective environments. They should not be treated as automatically synchronized. System backups may include or restore local data, depending on device settings and the final implementation.

3. Advertising, tracking, and permissions

The current design includes no third-party advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs, no use of advertising identifiers, and no features that request camera, microphone, location, contacts, or local-network access. We will check actual dependencies and permissions before release and update this notice if the design changes.

4. Device and platform services

Apple may process download, device diagnostic, crash, or other platform data under its policies and your device settings, and may make permitted reports available to the developer. This is separate from the game actively uploading learning records. This notice does not mean that Apple or the operating system processes no data. See your device privacy settings and Apple's Privacy Policy for relevant controls.

5. Support email

When a parent chooses to email support, the developer receives the sender's email address, message, and any voluntarily provided attachments to respond, investigate issues, and handle privacy requests. Please do not send a child's name, photo, school, home address, account passwords, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Messages pass through your email provider and our support email provider, QQ Mail, under their respective policies. We retain support information only as needed to handle the request and necessary follow-up, unless retention is required by law. You can use the same address to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information sent directly to support. Email providers may have separate backup and retention rules.

6. Managing and deleting records

Local learning records are managed on your device. The developer cannot retrieve or remotely delete them from a backend because no such backend is currently planned. Deleting the iOS app generally removes its local data; the system's “Offload App” action may keep documents and data. Clearing browser game site data may erase browser progress. System backups may retain older copies or restore records when a device is restored, so also review your backup settings. Exact instructions will be verified before release.

If you believe a child has sent unnecessary personal information to support, a parent or guardian should contact the address above so we can help with a deletion request.

7. This website and external links

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting a page sends the host a request that includes network information such as your IP address and browser information. GitHub may process requests and logs under its Privacy Statement. We cannot promise that the hosting provider keeps no logs.

We have added no advertising, analytics or tracking scripts, external fonts, embedded media, or contact forms to this site. The site does not actively read the game's local learning records. Visiting the website, opening external policy links, or sending email requires a network connection. External services handle data under their own policies.

8. Updates and contact

We will update this page and its date if the product design or data handling changes. The final notice before release must reflect the actual version. Developer: Peng Lin. Support and privacy contact: 540933120@qq.com.
