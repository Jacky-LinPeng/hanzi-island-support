给家长的一份说明

隐私说明

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 8 日

适用版本：1.0 与 1.1

本说明覆盖已发布的 1.0 和准备发布的 1.1。第 3 节广告数据处理仅适用于安装并使用 1.1 的用户；1.0 未集成广告 SDK。各版本的可用状态以 App Store 为准。网站与支持邮件部分在您访问本站或联系支持时适用。

汉字小岛由 Peng Lin 开发，面向 5～7 岁儿童及陪伴他们的家长，在 iPad 上提供简体中文、普通话识字学习。1.0 包含 1,500 个汉字、150 个单元和 750 个两字任务，免费下载，无内购或订阅；1.1 接入 Google AdMob 启动广告及地图页横幅广告。隐私问题请由家长或监护人联系 540933120@qq.com。

学习用途与内容纠错

本 App 主要用于辅助幼儿及小学低年级儿童学习汉字，建议由家长陪伴使用，不替代学校教学或专业教学评估。如您发现汉字、读音、词语、例句或其他内容存在错误，欢迎由家长或监护人通过 540933120@qq.com 指出并提供更正建议。请说明具体内容和所在课程；开发者会核实反馈，并通过后续更新更正和修复已确认的问题。反馈无需提供儿童的个人信息。

1. 账号与儿童信息

App 无需注册或登录，不要求儿童提供姓名、生日、照片、联系方式或其他身份信息。App 不提供聊天、公开个人资料或局域网多人功能。学习记录、支持联系、外部链接、重要设置及进度重置放在家长验证之后。

2. 设备本地记录

App 使用本机 IndexedDB 保存任务进度、练习与提示使用记录、声音及动态效果设置，用于继续学习、展示练习表现和恢复偏好。完整课程与绘本故事使用分别保存的进度。课程资源随 App 提供，学习可离线进行。App 没有开发者业务后端、账号服务、云同步或独立的学习行为分析 SDK，不向开发者上传学习记录。

iPad App 与浏览器版本中的数据保存在各自环境，不会自动互通。系统备份可能包含或恢复本地数据，具体取决于设备与备份设置；App 不提供跨设备恢复或同步服务。

3. 广告、追踪与设备权限

iOS 1.1 接入 Google Mobile Ads SDK，用于启动广告及地图页横幅广告。SDK 初始化前设置儿童年龄处理、G 级广告内容上限、关闭个性化广告及发布商第一方 ID；App 不请求 ATT 跟踪授权，不主动读取或传入 IDFA，也不把学习记录、姓名或联系方式传给广告服务。未集成 Firebase 或其他广告中介 SDK。这些设置不等同于广告服务完全不处理数据。

广告请求需要网络。依据 Google 的数据披露，SDK 可能处理 IP 地址（可用于推断大致位置）、诊断与性能信息、广告展示与交互数据，以及配置允许的设备或应用范围标识信息，用于广告投放、计量、防欺诈及服务改进。这些数据由广告服务处理，与保存在设备上的学习存档不同。

上述广告服务数据由 Google 按其服务及隐私政策处理，可能在其全球基础设施存储或处理。开发者不指定其单一存储地点或保留期限。请参阅 Google SDK 数据披露、Google 隐私政策及合作伙伴服务中的数据使用说明。App 不申请相机、麦克风、定位、联系人或局域网权限，不包含内购、订阅或收集儿童联系方式的表单。

4. 设备朗读与平台服务

普通话朗读由 AVSpeechSynthesizer 使用设备已安装的 Apple 简体中文（zh-CN）声音生成。App 不录制声音，不向开发者或语音服务器上传朗读文本，也不捆绑从 macOS 导出的系统语音录音。设备声音不可用时，家长可检查系统朗读声音设置，或使用文字提示继续学习。设备通过 Apple 获取系统声音所需的下载与管理由操作系统提供。

Apple 可能依据其政策及您的设备设置处理下载、设备诊断、崩溃或其他平台数据，并可能向开发者提供平台允许的报告。这与游戏主动上传学习记录不同。本说明不表示 Apple 或操作系统完全不处理数据。相关控制请查看设备隐私设置及 Apple 隐私政策。

5. 支持邮件

当家长主动发送支持邮件时，开发者会收到发件邮箱、邮件内容及自愿提供的附件，用于回复、排查问题及处理隐私请求。请不要发送儿童的姓名、照片、学校、住址、账号密码或其他不必要的敏感信息。

邮件会经过您使用的邮件服务及支持邮箱服务（QQ 邮箱），并受各自服务政策约束。我们仅在处理请求和必要后续事项所需期间保留支持信息，法律要求保留的情况除外。您可以联系同一邮箱，要求查阅、更正或删除直接发给支持的信息；邮件服务的备份与保留机制可能另有规则。

6. 管理与删除记录

家长可在对应学习页面的家长区重置记录，并通过第二次确认完成删除。完整课程的重置只删除完整课程进度；绘本故事中的重置按确认页面说明删除对应故事记录，其他学习进度保留。重置无法撤销。开发者没有业务服务器，无法查询或远程删除您的本机学习记录。

“删除 App”通常会移除其本机数据；系统的“卸载 App”会保留文稿与数据，与删除不同。清除浏览器的网站数据可能删除浏览器版本的进度。系统备份可能保留旧副本或在恢复设备时还原记录，请同时检查备份设置。

如您认为儿童向支持邮箱发送了不必要的个人信息，请由家长联系上述邮箱，以便我们协助处理删除请求。

7. 本支持网站与外部链接

App 中的外部支持链接位于家长验证之后，打开链接需要网络连接。本网站使用 GitHub Pages 托管。访问页面会向托管服务发送请求，包含 IP 地址、浏览器信息等网络请求数据。GitHub 可能依据其 隐私声明处理请求和日志；我们不能承诺托管方不保留日志。

本站未加入广告、分析或追踪脚本、外部字体、嵌入式媒体或联系表单。网站不主动读取游戏的本地学习记录。访问网站、打开外部政策链接或发送邮件需要网络连接；外部服务按各自政策处理数据。

8. 说明更新与联系

如产品功能或数据处理发生变化，我们会在本页更新内容和日期。开发者：Peng Lin；支持与隐私联系：540933120@qq.com。

A note for parents

Privacy notice

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Applies to versions 1.0 and 1.1

This notice covers released version 1.0 and the upcoming version 1.1. Advertising data handling in section 3 applies only when you install and use version 1.1; version 1.0 does not include an advertising SDK. Check the App Store for version availability. The website and support email sections apply whenever you visit this site or contact support.

Hanzi Island (汉字小岛：趣味识字) is developed by Peng Lin for children aged 5–7 with a parent or guardian, offering simplified Chinese learning with Mandarin narration on iPad. Version 1.0 includes 1,500 characters, 150 units, and 750 two-character lessons. It is free to download with no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Version 1.1 integrates Google AdMob app-open and map-screen banner ads. Adults can send privacy questions to 540933120@qq.com.

Learning support and corrections

This app primarily supports Chinese character learning for young children and early primary learners. We recommend using it with a parent or guardian; it does not replace school teaching or professional educational assessment. If you notice an error in a character, pronunciation, word, example sentence, or other content, a parent or guardian is welcome to report it and suggest a correction at 540933120@qq.com. Please identify the content and lesson. The developer will review feedback and correct confirmed issues in future updates. No personal information about the child is needed.

1. Accounts and children's information

The app requires no registration or login and does not ask children for a name, birth date, photo, contact details, or other identifying information. It has no chat, public profiles, or local-network multiplayer. Learning records, support contact, external links, advanced settings, and progress resets are behind an adult check.

2. Records on the device

The app uses on-device IndexedDB to save lesson progress, practice and hint-use records, and sound and motion settings to resume learning, show practice performance, and restore preferences. The full course and story experience store their progress separately. Course resources are bundled with the app for offline learning. The app has no developer-operated backend, account service, cloud sync, or separate learning-analytics SDK, and does not upload learning records to the developer.

The iPad app and browser version store data in their own environments without automatic synchronization. System backups may include or restore local data depending on device and backup settings. The app does not provide a cross-device restore or sync service.

3. Advertising, tracking, and permissions

The iOS 1.1 app integrates Google Mobile Ads SDK. Before initialization, it applies child age treatment and a G content rating limit, disables personalized ads and publisher first-party ID, and does not request ATT authorization. The app does not explicitly read or pass IDFA, learning records, names, or contact details to advertising services. No Firebase or mediation SDKs are integrated. These settings do not mean that the advertising service processes no data.

Ad requests require a network connection. Google discloses that its SDK may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), diagnostics, performance information, ad impressions and interactions, and device/app identifiers permitted by its configuration, for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and service improvement. This advertising-service processing is separate from learning records stored only on the device.

Google processes advertising-service data under its own policies, potentially using its global infrastructure. We do not specify a single storage location or retention period for Google. See Google SDK data disclosure, Google Privacy Policy, and Google partner-site data practices. The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, or local-network access and has no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or forms collecting children’s contact details.

4. Device narration and platform services

Mandarin narration uses AVSpeechSynthesizer with an installed Apple Simplified Chinese (zh-CN) voice. The app does not record audio, upload narration text to the developer or a speech server, or bundle system-speech recordings exported from macOS. If the device voice is unavailable, a parent can check the system speech voice settings or use text hints to continue. The operating system handles downloading and managing system voices from Apple.

Apple may process download, device diagnostic, crash, or other platform data under its policies and your device settings, and may make permitted reports available to the developer. This is separate from the game actively uploading learning records. This notice does not mean that Apple or the operating system processes no data. See your device privacy settings and Apple's Privacy Policy for relevant controls.

5. Support email

When a parent chooses to email support, the developer receives the sender's email address, message, and any voluntarily provided attachments to respond, investigate issues, and handle privacy requests. Please do not send a child's name, photo, school, home address, account passwords, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Messages pass through your email provider and our support email provider, QQ Mail, under their respective policies. We retain support information only as needed to handle the request and necessary follow-up, unless retention is required by law. You can use the same address to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information sent directly to support. Email providers may have separate backup and retention rules.

6. Managing and deleting records

Parents can reset records in the parent area for the relevant learning experience, with a second confirmation before deletion. A full-course reset deletes only full-course progress. Story resets delete the corresponding story records as described on the confirmation screen and preserve other learning progress. A reset cannot be undone. There is no developer-operated backend, so the developer cannot retrieve or remotely delete your local learning records.

“Delete App” generally removes local app data. “Offload App” keeps documents and data and is different from deletion. Clearing browser site data may erase browser progress. System backups may retain older copies or restore records when a device is restored, so also review your backup settings.

If you believe a child has sent unnecessary personal information to support, a parent or guardian should contact the address above so we can help with a deletion request.

7. This website and external links

External support links in the app are behind an adult check and require a network connection. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting a page sends the host a request that includes network information such as your IP address and browser information. GitHub may process requests and logs under its Privacy Statement. We cannot promise that the hosting provider keeps no logs.

We have added no advertising, analytics or tracking scripts, external fonts, embedded media, or contact forms to this site. The site does not actively read the game's local learning records. Visiting the website, opening external policy links, or sending email requires a network connection. External services handle data under their own policies.

8. Updates and contact

We will update this page and its date if product features or data handling change. Developer: Peng Lin. Support and privacy contact: 540933120@qq.com.
